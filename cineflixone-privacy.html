Privacy Policy — CineFlixOne

Last updated: 24 October 2026

CineFlixOne ("the app") is a free Android app developed by Mohammed Faisal (developer name: CineFlixOne). The app provides a simple interface for browsing movies and watching publicly available live TV streams through a web view of our website, cineflixone.blogspot.com. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal data.

2. App preferences stored on your device

The app and the website it displays may store small preferences locally on your device only — for example, the last TV channel you watched and whether you prefer Movies or Live TV mode — so the app can resume where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

3. Third-party content and services

Movies and live TV channels shown in the app are streamed from third-party websites and public stream providers. When you play content, those providers may process technical information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. We do not control those third-party services.

4. Advertising

The website displayed in the app may show advertisements served by third-party advertising networks (such as Monetag). Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, in line with their own privacy policies. No advertising identifier is collected by us directly.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience entertainment app. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or from any other user.

6. Permissions

The app requests only the internet access permission needed to load and play content. It does not access your files, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
