Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

CineBari has no sign-up and no accounts. We do not ask for, collect or sell your personal information.

Stored on your device only

Features like Continue Watching, My List, favorite TV channels and your last-watched channel are stored in your own browser's local storage, on your device. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them completely.

Third-party services

  • TMDB — provides movie and series information and images.
  • Video & TV providers — playback happens in embedded third-party players and public streams; those services may set their own cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies.
  • YouTube — trailers and some live channels play through YouTube embeds.
  • Advertising partners — to keep CineBari free, advertising partners (such as Monetag) may show non-intrusive ads on browsing pages — never inside the video player — and may use cookies to measure and personalize ads. You can block cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • Anonymous counters — we may use privacy-friendly counting to understand how many visitors we have; this does not identify you personally.

Children

CineBari includes a Kids Zone with family content, but the service as a whole is intended for general audiences; parents should supervise children's viewing as with any streaming service.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
